# Scrum meeting schedule and minutes template

No meeting attendance or historical minutes were provided. The schedule below is proposed; all future entries are templates until a meeting actually occurs.

## Proposed cadence

- **Daily stand-up:** 10–15 minutes on agreed working days; each person shares progress, next step, and blockers.
- **Sprint planning:** At the start of each sprint; confirm goal, capacity, owners, dependencies, and acceptance criteria.
- **Sprint review:** At sprint end; demonstrate only running work and record actual feedback.
- **Retrospective:** After review; discuss what helped, what did not, and specific actions for the next sprint.

## Daily stand-up structure

1. What changed since the last stand-up?
2. What will I work on next?
3. What is blocked or needs a decision?
